Senior Software Engineer · Google

Yanwu Xu, Ph.D.

Advancing fast, compact, and on-device generative media for image and video synthesis and editing.

I am a Senior Software Engineer at Google, where I focus on efficient and on-device generative models across text-to-image generation, generalist image editing, and video synthesis. Previously, I worked as a Senior Research Scientist at FinBone building generative tools for AI filmmaking workflows, and as a Research Scientist at Snap Inc. (Creative Vision) researching mobile-friendly image and video models.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Boston University in 2024, advised by Dr. Kayhan Batmanghelich and co-advised by Dr. Mingming Gong, with a dissertation on improving the efficiency of conditional generative models. Earlier, I earned an M.S. in Intelligent Systems from the University of Pittsburgh (2022) and completed multiple research internships and student researcher terms at Google (2022–2023).
